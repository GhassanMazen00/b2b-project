# Name options

Shortlisted for: short, pronounceable in Arabic and English, meaningful in both
Egypt and the Gulf, and not already owned by a large regional company.

| Name | Arabic | Meaning | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanad** ← recommended | سند | support, backing | Names the differentiator directly: the daily support. Short, warm, and understood identically in Cairo and Riyadh. |
| Nitaq | نطاق | scope, range — and literally "domain" | Clever double meaning for a web business. Slightly more technical in tone. |
| Rakiza | ركيزة | pillar, foundation | Suggests infrastructure you build on. Heavier, more corporate. |
| Jisr | جسر | bridge | Bridging business and technology. Common in branding, so less distinctive. |
| Mihwar | محور | axis, hub | Substantial and confident, but vaguer about what you do. |

**Going with Sanad.** Every mention in the site is the string `سند` / `Sanad`,
so changing it is one find-and-replace if you prefer another.

Check before committing: `sanad.tech`, `sanad.dev`, `sanadweb.com`,
`getsanad.com`, and the Instagram / TikTok handles.
