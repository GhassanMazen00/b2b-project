Questions

The ones we get every week

Anything else, ask before you commit to anything at all.

Why is the price lower than other studios?

Because the work takes less time, not because it is worth less. Modern tooling removes the repetitive parts, so a job that takes two months elsewhere takes us two weeks. We pass that on instead of keeping it.

Is the price final?

Yes. The quote is written and lists what is included. Anything extra is priced and agreed before it starts, never added at the end.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and the code is handed over. If you leave, we package everything up for you first.

Is the monthly plan compulsory?

No. Take the site and host it anywhere. Most owners would simply rather not deal with hosting, backups and updates, which is what the plan covers.

What does daily support actually mean?

A named person replies on WhatsApp, Sunday to Thursday, within 24 hours and usually much sooner. Small changes are included up to two hours a month. Larger work is quoted first.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes, across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and Qatar. Meetings are online and contracts are in US dollars. Gulf pricing is shown above when you switch market.
