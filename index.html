How we price

Most studios quote by the hour and finish when they finish. We publish the price, write the date down and carry the risk if the work takes longer than we thought. That is the whole difference, and it is the reason clients stay.

Questions

Anything else, ask before you commit to anything.

Ask a question

Why is the price lower than other studios?

Because the work takes less time, not because it is worth less. Modern tooling removes the repetitive parts, so a job that takes two months elsewhere takes us two weeks.

Is the price final?

Yes. The quote is written and lists what is included. Anything extra is priced and agreed before it starts, never added to the last invoice.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the code is handed to you. If you leave, we package everything up first.

Is the monthly plan compulsory?

No. Take the site and host it anywhere. Most owners would rather not deal with hosting, backups and updates, which is what the plan covers.

What does daily support mean?

A named person replies on WhatsApp, Sunday to Thursday, within 24 hours. Small changes are included up to two hours a month. Larger work is quoted first.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes, across the Gulf. Meetings are online and contracts are in US dollars. Gulf pricing differs from the list above and is quoted on request.
