Questions

The six we hear every week.

Why is the price lower than other studios?

Because the work takes less time. Modern tools remove the repetitive parts, so a job that takes two months elsewhere takes us two weeks, and we charge for the two weeks.

Is the price final?

Yes. The quote is written and lists what is included. Anything extra is priced and agreed before it starts.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and the code is handed over at the end.

Do I have to pay the $50 a month?

No. You can host it anywhere. The monthly care is for owners who would rather not handle hosting, backups and updates.

What does daily support mean?

A person answers on WhatsApp, Sunday to Thursday, within 24 hours and usually the same day.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes, in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and Qatar. Meetings are online and Gulf prices show when you switch the market above.
